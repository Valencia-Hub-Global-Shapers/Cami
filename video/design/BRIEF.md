# Camí showcase video: brief, look and script

Made following the Mortiflix explainer method (brief, look system, script,
carried transitions, an original score timed to the picture, a QC pass).

## Brief

- **What:** a 2 minute horizontal (1920x1080, 30 fps) showcase of Camí, for an
  event organised with the Global Shapers Ramallah Hub.
- **For whom:** event attendees: Shapers, partners, students.
- **The one takeaway:** Camí puts admission and scholarship routes for
  Palestinian students in one checked, shared place, and every hub can help it
  grow.
- **Tone:** professional and serious, with quiet hope. No voiceover. Music only.
- **Credit:** opens with "Global Shapers Valencia Hub presents". After that the
  platform is the subject. The end card credits the collaboration: Bridging
  Futures (the initiative), Ramallah Hub (leads it), Valencia Hub (built Camí).

## Look

- Palette (the app's own): cream `#FBF6EE`, sand `#F1E9D8`, ink `#211E19`,
  terracotta `#C1652F` (accent: the path), route green `#2F6F65`, muted
  `#4A4438`, faint `#8A8271`, border `#E6DDCC`.
- Type: Fraunces (headlines), IBM Plex Sans (body and UI), as in the app.
- Shape language: the Camí mark (two points, a dashed line) is the motif and
  the carrier of the cuts. Rounded cards (16 px), pills, thin borders, light grain.
- The context section sits on ink: the serious part. The light arrives with
  the platform.

## Music

An original score, synthesised from code (`music/score.py`), so there are no
licence questions: 72 BPM, one bar = 100 frames, so every scene starts on a
bar line. D minor under the context, lifting to F major at the turn, a soft
piano pulse under the platform, a fuller build under the possibilities, and a
held chord under the end card.
